01 — What we study

Research

Full research page →
I.

Speciation & Reproductive isolation

How reproductive barriers form and break down between closely related lineages, often through the use of hybrid zones.

II.

Floral evolution & Pollination biology

How floral traits evolve in response to pollinators, and how ecological interactions shape phenotypic and genomic divergence.

III.

Phylogeography & Conservation genetics

Using genetic variation to reconstruct biogeographic history, population structure, and the processes shaping diversity across landscapes.

We combine wet lab and bioinformatic approaches with a heavy dose of fieldwork.
